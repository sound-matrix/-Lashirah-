About her

An archer with a steady aim

Nandavaram Lashirah is a competitive archer in the Compound Girls division. Born on 6 November 2014, she has earned multiple championship gold medals and qualified for state and national tournaments. She trains at Ekalavya Sports Hub in Tellapur, Hyderabad, under Head Coach Srinivas Dontharaveni.

Training & mentorship

Built at Ekalavya Sports Hub

Lashirah does her daily physical conditioning and marksmanship training at Ekalavya Sports Hub in Tellapur, Hyderabad, an archery development facility equipped for international-standard compound bow training. She is coached directly by Head Coach Srinivas Dontharaveni, with academy leadership from Founder & CEO K. Anjaneyulu Goud, and competes in both Under-14 and Under-17 national divisions.

High-precision equipment calibration

Micro-adjusting compound bow draw weights, stabilizer balance, scope sights and arrow spine matching for sub-millimetre grouping.

Physical conditioning & postural stability

Targeted core, back and shoulder conditioning to keep her draw consistent over multi-distance scoring rounds.

Competitive mental toughness

Simulated matchplay elimination brackets, wind-drift adjustments and high-pressure shoot-off scenarios.
